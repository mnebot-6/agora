Child Safety Standards — Agora

Last updated: 2026-07-14

Agora is a community-management app for clubs, teams, and groups. It includes user-generated content (community chat and profiles) and is intended for users aged 13 and over. We are committed to keeping Agora free of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and we have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes our standards, in line with Google Play’s child safety standards policy.

1. Our commitment

  • We prohibit child sexual abuse material and any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, grooms, or otherwise harms minors.
  • We prohibit using Agora to solicit, produce, distribute, or facilitate access to such material or conduct.
  • Violations result in immediate content removal and account termination, and, where CSAM is involved, reporting to the appropriate authorities.

2. In-app reporting

Every user can report abusive content and users directly inside the App:

  • Report — Any community message or user profile can be reported from the App. Reports reach us for review.
  • Block — Any user can block another user, removing their content from view and preventing further contact.

You can also report child safety concerns at any time by emailing mnebotchirivella@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed and acted upon promptly.

3. How we respond

  • We review reports of child safety violations and remove violating content.
  • We suspend or permanently terminate accounts responsible for violations.
  • Where we identify apparent CSAM, we preserve the relevant evidence and report it to the competent authorities.

4. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws. As an operator based in Spain (EU), we report child sexual abuse material and related offenses to the relevant national and regional authorities, and, where applicable, to recognized reporting bodies such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). We cooperate with law-enforcement requests as required by law.

5. Age of use

Agora is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, whichever is higher). See our Privacy Policy for how we handle data belonging to minors.

6. Point of contact

Child safety point of contact: mnebotchirivella@gmail.com

We aim to acknowledge child safety reports as quickly as possible and to take action without undue delay.
